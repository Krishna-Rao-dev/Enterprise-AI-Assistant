## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines paid time off (PTO), sick leave, casual leave, and leave of absence practices for Northwind Technologies employees in India and the United States. It applies to regular full-time employees unless a local addendum or employment agreement provides a more generous statutory entitlement. Contractors should use the separate Contractor Onboarding Guide, HR-HBK-002, for engagement-specific time-off arrangements.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

Employees receive **24 working days of annual paid leave per completed calendar year**. The entitlement is credited monthly at 2 days per month. New joiners receive a prorated balance based on their start date. The leave year runs from January 1 through December 31.

| Leave category | Annual guideline | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Annual PTO / earned leave | 24 working days | Planned rest and personal commitments |
| Sick leave | 8 working days | Medical recovery; manager may request documentation for absences over 3 consecutive working days |
| Bereavement leave | Up to 5 working days | Immediate family member |
| Parental leave | Per local law and approved HR guidance | Contact HR for country-specific details |

Annual PTO is intended to be used during the year. Up to 5 unused days may carry forward into the next calendar year, subject to local law. Any balance above the carry-forward limit is handled according to applicable law and the employee's local employment terms.

## 3. Request and approval process

Submit planned PTO in the HR portal at least 5 working days before the first day away. For absences of 3 or more consecutive working days, submit at least 10 working days in advance when practicable. The direct manager approves or declines the request within 3 working days. The manager should consider delivery commitments, team coverage, and fairness across the team.

For sudden illness or emergencies, notify the manager as soon as reasonably possible and record the absence in the portal by the end of the first day back. If the portal displays `ERR-4021`, open an IT Service Desk ticket using category `HRIS > Leave Balance`; do not create a duplicate request.

## 4. Extended leave and leave of absence

A leave of absence lasting more than 10 consecutive working days requires HR review. Contact the HR Operations Partner before making non-refundable travel arrangements. HR will confirm documentation, benefits continuation, and payroll treatment based on country, employment status, and applicable law.

## 5. Responsibilities and exceptions

Employees are responsible for maintaining accurate requests and handover notes. Managers must not pressure employees to work while on approved leave except for a genuine emergency, and any exception must be escalated to the department head and HR. HR Operations owns policy interpretation. Questions should be sent to `people-ops@northwind.example` with subject `Leave Policy Question`.

This policy supersedes HR-POL-000, Leave and Paid Time Off Policy v1.0, effective April 1, 2026. Where local law grants a greater entitlement, local law prevails.
