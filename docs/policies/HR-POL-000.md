## 1. Purpose and scope

This archived policy described paid time off (PTO), sick leave, and leave-of-absence practices for Northwind Technologies employees in India and the United States. It applied to regular full-time employees, subject to local law and the employee's signed employment terms. This version is retained for historical reference and is no longer the governing policy.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement in version 1.0

Under the rules effective January 15, 2025, employees received **18 working days of annual paid leave per calendar year**, credited at 1.5 days per month. New joiners received a prorated balance based on their start date. The leave year ran from January 1 through December 31.

| Leave category | Annual guideline in v1.0 | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Annual PTO / earned leave | 18 working days | Planned rest and personal commitments |
| Sick leave | 8 working days | Medical recovery |
| Bereavement leave | Up to 3 working days | Immediate family member |
| Parental leave | Per local law | Contact HR for country-specific guidance |

Employees could carry forward up to 3 unused annual leave days into the following year, subject to local law. This carry-forward rule is also superseded. Employees must not rely on this document to calculate their current balance.

## 3. Request and approval process under v1.0

Planned leave was to be submitted in the HR portal at least 3 working days before the first day away. The direct manager was expected to respond within 5 working days. Sudden illness or emergencies were to be reported to the manager as soon as possible, with the portal record completed upon return.

For portal failures, employees were advised to email `people-ops@northwind.example`. The current process uses the IT Service Desk ticket category `HRIS > Leave Balance` for error `ERR-4021`; follow HR-POL-001 for current instructions.

## 4. Historical extended leave guidance

A leave of absence longer than 15 consecutive working days required HR review. The former guidance suggested contacting HR at least 5 working days before the leave where possible. This threshold and the older request timelines have been replaced by the current policy.

## 5. Status and records

**Status: SUPERSEDED.** Effective April 1, 2026, HR-POL-001 replaced this document and changed the annual PTO entitlement from 18 to 24 working days, increased the carry-forward limit from 3 to 5 days, and updated request timelines. The current policy is the authoritative source for present-day requests, approvals, and balances.

This document remains available so HR Operations can explain historical balances and audit decisions made before April 1, 2026. If a question concerns an earlier absence, include the absence dates and employee ID in a request to `people-ops@northwind.example`. Do not apply the archived numbers to a current-year entitlement.
