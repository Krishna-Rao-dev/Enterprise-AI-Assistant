## 1. Purpose and access

This runbook defines how HR personnel receive, triage, investigate, and close employee conduct cases. Access is restricted to the `hr` group. Managers may submit concerns through the designated HR intake channel, but this document and investigation notes must not be shared with managers, employees, contractors, or other groups. Use the minimum information necessary at each stage.

## 2. Intake and triage

Record each report in the case management system within one business day. Use case identifiers in the format `HRCASE-YYYY-NNNN`, for example `HRCASE-2026-0147`. Record the date received, reporter's preferred contact method, involved business unit, alleged conduct category, immediate safety concerns, and any known evidence. Do not characterize an allegation as a finding at intake.

| Severity | Examples | Initial response target |
|---|---|---|
| P1: urgent | Threats, credible safety risk, retaliation in progress | Same business day |
| P2: high | Harassment allegation, suspected fraud, repeated serious misconduct | 2 business days |
| P3: standard | Attendance, respectful-workplace concern, policy non-compliance | 5 business days |

For credible threats or immediate danger, contact Corporate Security or emergency services as appropriate, then notify the HR Director. If the report concerns a senior executive or an HR team member, route it to the Chief People Officer or designated independent counsel to avoid conflicts of interest.

## 3. Investigation controls

Assign an investigator who has no reporting-line or personal conflict with the parties. Prepare an investigation plan with questions, evidence sources, interview order, and an estimated completion date. Preserve relevant messages, access logs, and documents under the company's retention process. Do not access personal accounts or devices without approved legal authority and documented authorization.

Interview participants separately. Explain confidentiality expectations without promising absolute secrecy. Record factual observations and distinguish direct evidence from hearsay. The target is 15 business days for standard cases; complex cases may take longer, but the case owner must document the reason and provide a status update every 10 business days.

## 4. Findings and corrective action

Use the outcome labels `substantiated`, `unsubstantiated`, or `inconclusive`. The investigator submits findings and evidence references to the HR Director. Corrective action is decided separately from the fact-finding report and should be proportionate, consistent with precedent, and compliant with local law. HR must consult Legal before termination for alleged misconduct, protected leave, accommodation-related issues, or any case with credible retaliation risk.

## 5. Confidentiality and closure

Store case material in the restricted HR case repository, not in shared team drives. Access is logged. Never include sensitive witness details in a general employee relations tracker. Close the case only after the decision, communication plan, required approvals, and follow-up actions are documented. Retain records for 7 years unless Legal issues a hold or local law requires a longer period.
