## 1. Who is covered

This handbook summarizes core benefits for regular Northwind Technologies employees. Eligibility can vary by country, employment type, start date, and local law. **Independent contractors are not eligible for employee benefits described here** unless a signed agreement explicitly says otherwise. Contractors should refer to HR-HBK-002 for onboarding and engagement terms.

## 2. Health and wellbeing

Employees in India are enrolled in the company group medical plan from their start date, subject to insurer enrollment requirements. The standard plan provides up to ₹5,00,000 of annual family coverage. Employees may purchase optional parent coverage during the annual enrollment window, subject to insurer terms and payroll deductions.

US employees receive medical, dental, and vision plan options. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following the employee's start date, provided enrollment is completed within 30 days. Employees who miss the initial enrollment window must generally wait until annual open enrollment unless they experience a qualifying life event.

The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) provides confidential counseling and wellbeing referrals. Access details are available on the People Portal under `Benefits > Wellbeing`. Managers do not receive individual EAP usage details.

## 3. Retirement and savings

| Location | Program | General information |
|---|---|---|
| India | EPF, where applicable | Employer and employee contributions follow statutory rules |
| US | 401(k) plan | Eligible employees may contribute through payroll; company match is described in the plan notice |

Plan documents and applicable law control if this summary differs from official provider terms. HR does not provide personal tax advice.

## 4. Learning and home-office support

Employees may request up to ₹30,000 per calendar year for approved role-related learning in India, or $1,200 in the US. Requests require manager approval before purchase and a receipt within 30 days. Courses must relate to the employee's current role or an agreed development plan.

Eligible employees may also claim a one-time home-office setup reimbursement of up to ₹15,000 in India or $500 in the US during their first 90 days. Submit claims through the expense platform using category `People > Home Office`. Standard expense controls still apply.

## 5. Family and life events

Employees should notify HR promptly after a marriage, birth, adoption, or other qualifying event that may affect benefits enrollment. HR Operations will explain the required documentation and applicable enrollment deadline. Do not email medical records or identity documents to a broad distribution list; use the secure People Portal upload.

## 6. Questions and updates

For benefit eligibility or enrollment help, contact `people-ops@northwind.example` with subject `Benefits Enrollment`. The handbook is a summary, not a contract. Benefit providers, local law, and signed employment terms take precedence where they differ. Updates are published on the People Portal and announced through the monthly People Operations bulletin.
