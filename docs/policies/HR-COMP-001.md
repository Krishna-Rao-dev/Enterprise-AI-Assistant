## 1. Purpose and handling

This restricted reference supports compensation planning for Northwind Technologies' approximately 400 employees across India and the United States. Access is limited to members of `hr_managers` and `executives`. Do not paste salary bands into general Slack channels, employee-facing FAQs, recruiting presentations, or documents accessible to `all_employees`. Individual compensation records remain in the HRIS and are not included here.

## 2. Indicative annual base salary bands

Amounts below are planning ranges, not guaranteed offers. India figures are in INR lakhs per annum (LPA); US figures are in USD per annum. The hiring manager and HR Compensation Partner must validate level, location, and internal equity before any offer is approved.

| Job family / level | India range | US range |
|---|---:|---:|
| Software Engineer I | ₹12–20 LPA | $95,000–$135,000 |
| Software Engineer II | ₹20–32 LPA | $130,000–$175,000 |
| Senior Software Engineer | ₹30–48 LPA | $160,000–$215,000 |
| Engineering Manager | ₹42–65 LPA | $185,000–$245,000 |
| Product / Design Specialist | ₹16–35 LPA | $110,000–$190,000 |
| Finance / Operations Analyst | ₹8–18 LPA | $70,000–$115,000 |

The ranges are reviewed twice a year. The next scheduled review is January 2027. They exclude variable incentives, equity, sign-on bonuses, benefits, and employer contributions.

## 3. Annual review cycle

The performance year runs from January 1 to December 31. Managers submit ratings and proposed salary changes in the HRIS during the February review window. HR Compensation checks for pay equity, compression, promotion alignment, and budget availability. The CFO and Chief People Officer approve the final annual pool; the CEO approves exceptions above the executive compensation committee's delegated limit.

A standard merit adjustment is not automatic. Promotion-related adjustments must include the new role scope and a written calibration rationale. Retention adjustments require evidence of business risk and must not be used to bypass the normal performance process.

## 4. Approval controls

| Change type | Required approval |
|---|---|
| Within band, no promotion | Department VP + HR Compensation |
| Promotion or band change | Department VP + HR Director |
| Above-band exception | CFO + Chief People Officer |
| Executive compensation change | CEO + designated board committee |

Any exception request must include a documented business case, proposed effective date, budget owner, and comparison against the employee's current band. Never send salary information to personal email accounts.

## 5. Audit and escalation

HR Compensation retains approval records for 7 years or longer where local law requires. Suspected disclosure should be reported immediately to the Chief People Officer and IT Security. This document is not an employee-facing promise of pay and must not be used to infer any individual's salary.
