## 1. How much paid time off do I receive?

Under the current Leave and Paid Time Off Policy, HR-POL-001, regular full-time employees receive 24 working days of annual PTO per calendar year, credited at 2 days per month. You may hear colleagues call this annual leave, earned leave, vacation, or PTO. In the HR portal, these terms refer to the same annual paid time-off balance unless a local addendum explicitly separates them.

The older policy HR-POL-000 listed 18 days. That document is superseded and should not be used to calculate current entitlement.

## 2. Is sick time part of annual PTO?

No. The standard guideline is 8 working days of sick leave per year, separate from annual PTO. If you are ill, notify your manager as soon as reasonably possible. For an absence longer than 3 consecutive working days, your manager may request supporting documentation consistent with local law and privacy requirements.

## 3. What if I need an extended break?

A leave of absence lasting more than 10 consecutive working days requires HR review. Examples may include a longer personal situation, medical recovery, or a family need. Contact HR Operations before finalizing plans so the team can explain documentation, benefits continuation, and payroll implications for your location.

## 4. How early should I request time off?

| Situation | Recommended timing |
|---|---|
| Planned PTO | At least 5 working days in advance |
| 3 or more consecutive working days | At least 10 working days in advance where practicable |
| Sudden illness or emergency | Notify your manager as soon as possible; update the portal by your first day back |

Managers should respond within 3 working days. If a request is declined, ask your manager about alternative dates or coverage options.

## 5. What if my leave balance looks wrong?

Check that the dates, leave type, and approval status are correct. If the portal shows `ERR-4021`, open an IT Service Desk ticket under `HRIS > Leave Balance`. Include the date of the issue and a screenshot with personal details minimized. Do not send passwords or medical records in the ticket.

## 6. Can unused PTO roll over?

Up to 5 unused annual PTO days may carry forward to the next calendar year, subject to local law and employment terms. Balances above the limit are handled according to the applicable rules for your location. Ask HR Operations if you are unsure how a local rule applies.

## 7. Where can I get help?

Email `people-ops@northwind.example` with subject `Leave Policy Question`, or use the People Portal help form. Contractors should consult HR-HBK-002 and their SOW because employee PTO entitlements do not automatically apply to contractor engagements.
