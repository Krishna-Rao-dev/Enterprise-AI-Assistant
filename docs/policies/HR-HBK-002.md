## 1. Purpose and audience

This guide helps independent contractors and vendor-assigned personnel begin work with Northwind Technologies. It is available to the `contractors` and `hr` groups. Contractors should follow the statement of work (SOW), master services agreement, and local legal requirements if any instruction here conflicts with a signed contract. This guide does not create an employment relationship.

## 2. Before the first working day

The sponsoring manager submits a contractor request at least 7 business days before the planned start date. HR Vendor Operations verifies the vendor, SOW dates, work location, and sponsor. The contractor must complete identity and right-to-work checks applicable to their location, sign confidentiality and acceptable-use acknowledgments, and provide payment details through the approved vendor process.

| Requirement | Owner | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Approved SOW and purchase order | Sponsoring manager / Procurement | Before access request |
| Identity and compliance checks | HR Vendor Operations | Before start |
| Confidentiality and security training | Contractor / IT Security | By Day 1 |
| Account and equipment request | Sponsor / IT Service Desk | 3 business days before start |

## 3. Accounts and equipment

The sponsor opens an IT Service Desk request using catalog item `CONTRACTOR-ACCESS`. Include the contractor's legal name, company email, sponsor, project code, start date, end date, and requested systems. Access is provisioned for the minimum duration and scope needed. Contractor accounts must not be shared, and personal devices may be used only when explicitly approved by IT Security.

If an account request returns `ERR-4037`, verify that the SOW and purchase order are active, then reference the original ticket when contacting Vendor Operations. Do not create a second identity record to bypass the validation.

## 4. Working arrangements and time away

Contractors coordinate planned time away directly with the sponsoring manager and the vendor contact. Contractor fees, invoicing, public holidays, and any paid or unpaid time away are governed by the SOW and applicable agreement, not the employee PTO policy. Contractors should notify the sponsor as early as possible when an absence affects a delivery milestone.

## 5. Training, conduct, and confidentiality

Contractors must complete security awareness and code-of-conduct training before receiving production access. Company information may be used only for the contracted purpose. Do not copy source code, customer data, credentials, or internal documents into personal storage or unapproved AI services. Report suspected phishing or accidental disclosure to the IT Service Desk immediately.

## 6. End of engagement

The sponsoring manager must confirm the end date and initiate offboarding at least 2 business days before the engagement ends, where practicable. IT disables accounts at the approved end time, collects company equipment, and removes group memberships. Contractors must return or securely delete company information and certify completion if requested. Extension requests require an updated SOW and approval before the existing end date.
